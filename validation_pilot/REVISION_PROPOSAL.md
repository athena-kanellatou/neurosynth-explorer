# Protocol revision proposal A1 - 28 September 2026

**Status:** Proposed before eligibility screening. The prospective validation protocol has not been registered or frozen. No candidate article has been marked included/excluded.

## Why narrow the scope?

The broad PubMed pilot returned 1,024 emotion-regulation and 2,590 reward arm-level records. A fully screened review at that scope requires substantial resources. A narrower, task-oriented validation question would compare **cognitive reappraisal** with **monetary reward/incentive** experiments, recognizing that this no longer represents all emotion regulation or all reward paradigms.

## Narrow PubMed pilot

| Arm | Exact search concept | Hits |
|---|---|---:|
| Cognitive reappraisal | `"cognitive reappraisal" OR "emotion reappraisal"` in title/abstract, plus fMRI terms | 135 |
| Monetary reward | `"monetary incentive delay" OR "monetary reward"` in title/abstract, plus fMRI terms | 372 |

Date range: 1 January 2019 through 28 September 2026. The complete field-tagged strings and query metadata are in `narrow_search_log.json`. The two PubMed result sets have **507 unique PMID** and zero cross-arm duplicate PMID in this retrieval. `narrow_full_queue.csv` records each title, DOI when available, date, journal, arm membership and PubMed URL. Every record is `NOT SCREENED`.

## Decision required before confirmatory work

1. Validate that the narrower concepts capture the intended tasks by reviewing a sample of abstracts and search terms **without using reported brain locations as a selection criterion**.
2. Add a second bibliographic database, deduplicate records, and record the full search strings and counts.
3. Fix operational eligibility and contrast-selection rules, access to full texts, review staffing, ALE software settings and feasibility criteria.
4. Then freeze/date the revised protocol before the first eligibility decision. Keep this proposal and the broad pilot as an audit trail.

This is a feasibility finding, not a result about neural activity or study eligibility. PubMed counts may change with indexing.
